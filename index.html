FAQ
Where is my data stored?

In HealthKit, on your device. Dipsago uploads nothing, needs no account, and ships without analytics or ads.

Which devices are supported?

Any iPhone on iOS 26 or later, any Apple Watch on watchOS 26 or later. Widgets run on both.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging, reminders, and charts all run on your device, so Dipsago works without a connection.

How much does it cost?

Dipsago is free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

Can I log a drink from earlier today?

Yes. Pick a date and time to log something you drank earlier.

Can I edit or delete an entry?

Yes. Change the amount, drink or time, or delete the entry. Entries from other Health apps stay locked, Apple does not let us touch those.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

The short version

Dipsago does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no servers behind this app, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

What Dipsago does with your data

Dipsago is a water tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It reads and writes your water intake through Apple Health (HealthKit).

All water entries live in Apple's HealthKit database. Dipsago only asks HealthKit for the entries it needs to show you.

To show your data quickly, and so the widgets work, Dipsago keeps small temporary copies of today's water totals and pending entries on your device, for example in the shared app group the widgets use. These snapshots exist only on your device, no other app can read them, and they are removed when you delete Dipsago. Dipsago never sends anything anywhere.

Data on your device

The app stores a few small preferences and local log files so it can remember things like your daily water goal and which screen you last looked at. These files live in the app's own container and are removed when you delete the app. They never leave your device.

Health data

Health data is handled by Apple's HealthKit, not by Dipsago. That means:

  • Your water entries are protected by the same privacy controls Apple applies to all health data.
  • Dipsago has no access to your data once it leaves your device. It has no server to receive it.
  • If you delete an entry in Dipsago, it is removed from HealthKit. To remove everything, delete the entries in the Health app or revoke Dipsago's access in Health settings.
  • If you have iCloud Health sync enabled, Apple may sync your Health data to your iCloud account. That happens through Apple's own systems, not through Dipsago.

Notifications

Dipsago can send local reminders to help you drink water. These are created on your device and never leave it. No notification data is sent anywhere.

Third parties

There are none. Dipsago contains no third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking software.

Children

The app is safe for anyone to use. Since no data is collected, there is nothing to protect here.

Changes to this policy

If this app ever changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated before that change ships. You can check this page at any time to see the current version.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

All water entries are stored in Apple Health on your device. Dipsago does not have its own database and does not send data anywhere.

I deleted an entry by accident. Can you restore it?

Dipsago cannot restore entries because it has no copy of your data. Check the Health app on your iPhone: entries deleted in Dipsago are removed from HealthKit, but anything synced through iCloud may still show up in Health's own history.

Dipsago is not showing data from my Apple Watch.

Open the Health app on your iPhone, tap your profile picture, and check that water intake appears under Health Access. If Dipsago has access, make sure both devices are online and give them a moment to sync.

How do I delete all my data?

Remove Dipsago's access in the Health app (Health > profile > Apps > Dipsago) and delete the app. Water entries you added will stay in HealthKit unless you delete them there as well.
